How it works

Three steps, no app install

MechFind runs right in your browser as part of the Ghostroute suite. Here's the exact flow.

01

Allow your location

A one-time permission prompt so MechFind can measure real distance to nearby Auto-repair shops — not a rough guess. GPS must be on at the device level, not just in the browser.

02

We scan the radius

Verified mechanics and auto repair shops in your area are pulled up and sorted closest-to-you first, measured with ratings from our Oracle Detection Systems, accompanied with other drivers that use MechFind.

03

Call or view the mechanic

Tap to call the shop directly, or open their profile for address and directions. No middleman, no waiting on a chat reply.
